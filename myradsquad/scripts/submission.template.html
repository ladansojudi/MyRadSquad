The job

Design the first Squad+ experience for US teens (13–17): onboarding (feature screens, a 13+ gate, "why are you here?") → paywall A/B → Home → one generative AI feature, as a working build on a phone.

Insight

A quiet squad feels dead. Teens pay for what makes it feel alive.

Free vs Squad+

Free: 5 people. Squad+: 12 people, premium fun and one AI feature.

Price

$3.99/week or $29.99/year, 3-day free trial.

How we got there

Question → answer → source

User

RQ1What makes a group feel alive?

Found

Small daily rituals: streaks, one daily prompt, a Home Screen widget. But they backfire when they feel forced.

  • DeskBits of Freedom survey (ages 13–21): 86% keep streaks, 42% would remove them. ↗
  • CompetitorsBeReal breaks silence with a daily prompt, Locket with a widget; BeReal later lost users. ↗

RQ2What do teens pay for?

Found

Fun, early features, AI tools, and things they don't want to lose (streaks).

  • DeskSnap: 30M subscribers; the AI/AR tier Lens+ grew ~600% this year. ↗
  • Desk25% of young streak users paid €0.99 to restore a broken streak. ↗

Onboarding

RQ3How do competitors show value before paying?

Found

Only Locket shows the benefit before sign-up; Locket and BeReal explain empty screens, with no paywall in the core flow.

  • CompetitorsCompetitor matrix: Locket shows the widget before sign-up; BeReal's first screen is just a logo, Snapchat's just sign-in buttons.
  • DeskPartiful lets guests RSVP without an account. ↗

RQ4Does a new user join by invite, or start their own squad?

Assumed

Unknown. Assumption A1: most arrive by invite; a "create + invite" flow for the rest.

  • CompanyAsk the company.
  • CompetitorsBeReal invites via QR, link and "skip".

Paywall

RQ5Weekly or annual, and how many pay after the trial?

Found

Most pick weekly, but annual subscribers stay; nearly half of trials cancel on day one.

  • DeskAdapty: 62% of revenue is weekly; 44% of trials cancel in 24h. ↗
  • DeskRevenueCat: year-one retention 44% annual vs 3% weekly. ↗

RQ6Can an under-18 pay alone?

Partial

Often not: with Ask to Buy on, a parent must approve. How it works with trials is unclear.

  • DeskApple Support: approval required; unanswered requests expire in 24h. ↗

AI

RQ7When does AI feel creepy?

Found

When it knows something it shouldn't, asks about people in photos, or can't be turned off.

  • DeskSnapchat My AI coverage: location, photo questions, can't remove it. ↗
  • DeskCommon Sense: 1 in 3 teen AI users felt uncomfortable. ↗

RQ8What data can the AI see?

Assumed

Unknown. Assumption A3: only the squad chat it's on in; no DMs, location or faces; visible to all.

  • CompanyAsk the company.

RQ9What does AI do in apps like this?

Found

Three ways: a bot in the chat (risky), creating fun content, and private catch-up summaries.

  • DeskDiscord shut down its chat bot Clyde in 2023 after offensive replies. ↗
  • DeskApple Genmoji makes an emoji from a typed description. ↗
  • DeskWhatsApp summarises unread messages; off by default, only you see it. ↗

Home

RQ10What should a new user see on Home?

Found

One next step, why an empty area is empty, and the squad itself.

  • CompetitorsCompetitor matrix: Locket and BeReal explain empty screens and show one step.
  • DeskOn BeReal you can't see friends' posts until you post yours. ↗
  • DeskGeneva splits a group into chat, audio and video rooms. ↗

RQ11When does an app or AI feel spammy?

Found

When it comes too often, sits above friends, and can't be removed.

  • DeskCommon Sense: half of teens 11–17 get 237+ notifications a day. ↗
  • DeskSnapchat's pinned, unremovable My AI got 75% one-star reviews. ↗
  • CompetitorsCompetitor matrix: BeReal ties notifications to one daily moment; Snapchat asks mid sign-up, before any value.

Solution

Show the squad alive first, ask for money second; one AI spark a day brings a quiet squad back.

13+ gate (at sign-in)→Feature screens→What are you here for?→Paywall A/B→Home→Spark

The age gate comes first, not third: a teen under 13 should not see the product pitch, and the FTC asks for the age screen before any personal data.

Key idea · S1 · AI feature · HMW3

Squad Spark: when the chat goes quiet, one spark a day gets the squad talking.

What it does

Leaves one "spark" in the squad chat: a question, a mini game or a photo task. "Drop this week's funniest moment", "Who wakes up first? Vote".

When

Only after the chat has been quiet for a while; at most once a day. Never in a live squad.

State 1 · the member who turns it on

Picks a vibe and sees Spark's four rules before adding it (needs Squad+).

State 2 · the rest of the squad

Sees "Maya added Spark" with Mute, and the spark inside their squad's card on Home.

💬Reads only this chat
✕No DMs, faces or location
🔔Max once a day, when quiet
🔕Anyone can mute or remove it

Not a streak: no counter, nothing to lose if you skip it. Two sparks in a row with no answer and it takes a break. Why: My AI backlash and notification overload (RQ7, RQ11); daily rituals work but backfire when forced (RQ1).

What we design

S2 · Feature screens · HMW2

A live squad preview before any ask.

Three swipeable pages, one graphic and one idea each: your squad is already talking, rooms to hang out, focus and play in, and Spark so the chat never dies. Locket shows value before sign-up.

S3 · What are you here for? · HMW1

One tap: hang out, study, share moments.

Personalises the paywall's headline and art.

S4 · Paywall A / B · HMW2

Two paywalls: A fun and AI, B the squad (12 seats).

A leads with what they came for. Annual pre-selected, shown monthly, clear close (like Locket Gold). Metric: day-7 paid conversion per paywall view.

S5 · Home · HMW1

Who's here, today's spark, one next step.

Live squads first: who's here and the room friends started (join it or start your own). Quiet squads below, with Spark's spark inside their card. If empty: why + invite (RQ10). No streaks.

S6 · Ask a parent · HMW4

"Waiting for approval"; keep using free meanwhile.

Ask to Buy can take up to 24h.
